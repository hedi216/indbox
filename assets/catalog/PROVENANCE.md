# Catalogue imagery

The official `../logo.png` and `../bckgimg.png` were supplied by the project owner and preserved unchanged.

Seven catalogue photographs were created with the built-in **imagegen** tool, then converted to 900-pixel WebP for the site. These are presentation images for seeded sample products, not photographs of verified commercial inventory. Replace or enrich them with real product photography in the admin before publishing the commercial catalogue.

Saved assets and exact subject prompts:

| File | Subject |
|---|---|
| `coffret.webp` | An exquisite matte black rigid gift box with a fine gold rim, lid resting partly open, black tissue inside |
| `shopping.webp` | Two luxury paper shopping bags, one ivory and one black, with gold rope handles |
| `kraft.webp` | Natural kraft paper shopping bags with twisted paper handles, one large and one small |
| `stickers.webp` | A roll of circular black product labels with simple gold geometric decoration, and a small sheet of ivory labels |
| `food.webp` | Premium kraft food packaging collection: a burger clamshell box and a white pastry box with clear window |
| `cosmetic.webp` | Luxury black perfume carton and cream cosmetic gift box with gold foil edges |
| `pouch.webp` | Two standing resealable doypack pouches, matte black and natural kraft, with small transparent windows |

Each built-in generation used this shared prompt, with its subject and filename inserted:

> Use case: product-mockup. Create one square professional ecommerce product photograph. Subject: [SUBJECT]. Warm pale ivory seamless studio background, soft directional light, believable paper fibers and folds, gentle natural shadows, three-quarter view, centered full product with generous margins. Premium packaging aesthetic. No text, no lettering, no logo, no watermark. Save for catalogue asset named [NAME].

No generated image replaced the official IN-D-BOX logo. Multiple demo products may share a representative photograph.
